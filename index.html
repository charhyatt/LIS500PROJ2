Welcome

Welcome to my project website. This website explores ideas related to implicit bias, intersectionality, and representation in technology. The goal is to provide useful information and resources for students who are learning about these topics for the first time.

Why This Topic Matters

Technology plays a major role in everyday life. It influences communication, education, jobs, and access to information. Learning about fairness and bias can help people understand how technology affects different communities and experiences.
