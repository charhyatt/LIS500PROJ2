Welcome

Welcome to my project website. This website was created to explore topics related to implicit bias, equity, and technology. The goal is to provide information that is easy to understand for students who may be learning about these subjects for the first time.

images/homepage.jpg

Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because technology is becoming a bigger part of everyday life. Apps, websites, and artificial intelligence influence many decisions and experiences. Learning about bias and fairness can help people create better technology and make more informed decisions.

What You Will Find Here

  • Information about implicit bias
  • Resources for learning about intersectionality
  • A profile of a technology leader promoting equity
  • Links to additional educational resources
