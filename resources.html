What is Implicit Bias?

Implicit bias is a bias that someone may have without realizing it. These ideas can influence decisions and judgments even when people are trying to be fair.

What is Intersectionality?

Intersectionality is the idea that people have many different identities that shape their experiences, such as race, gender, class, and disability.

My Reflection

After learning about implicit bias, I realized that everyone has experiences that shape their opinions and assumptions. Being aware of those assumptions is important because it can help people make better decisions and treat others more fairly. I also learned that people's experiences cannot always be understood through just one part of their identity. The idea of intersectionality helps explain why different people may experience the same situation in different ways.
